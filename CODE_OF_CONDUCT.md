# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the GitHub Activity Graph Populator community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives. Whether you are reporting a bug, proposing a feature, improving documentation, submitting code, or simply using the project, you are part of this community.

We pledge to foster an environment where everyone can participate openly, collaborate constructively, and focus on building useful, creative, and reliable open-source software.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Being respectful, inclusive, and considerate in all interactions.
- Using welcoming and professional language.
- Respecting differing opinions, experiences, and approaches to software development.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully.
- Providing actionable, well-reasoned feedback on issues, pull requests, and code reviews.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respecting contributors' time, effort, and boundaries.
- Being open to learning from mistakes and helping others improve.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct based on personal characteristics or identity.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained hostile behavior.
- Publishing private or personally identifying information without explicit permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, sexualized harassment, or inappropriate sexual content.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussions or repeatedly engaging in bad-faith arguments.
- Misusing issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project resources to target or harass individuals.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional, collaborative environment.

Disagreement about implementation details, design decisions, project priorities, or technical approaches is acceptable. Personal attacks and disrespectful conduct are not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and community interactions, including:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Code reviews, commits, and contribution-related communications.
- Project documentation and other community-maintained resources.
- Public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

This Code also applies when project-related interactions occur outside GitHub, provided there is a reasonable connection to the project or its community.

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards described in this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers are expected to:

- Communicate community standards clearly and consistently.
- Review reports of unacceptable behavior fairly and promptly.
- Respect the privacy of individuals involved in reported incidents.
- Take appropriate corrective action based on the nature and severity of the behavior.
- Apply these standards consistently, regardless of a contributor's role or experience.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, commits, and other contributions that violate this Code. They may also temporarily restrict or permanently revoke participation in project spaces when necessary.

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainer privately through GitHub.

You can reach the maintainer through the repository:

https://github.com/freaker2k7/github-activity-graph-populator

Please include, when possible:

- A description of the incident.
- Links to relevant issues, pull requests, comments, or other evidence.
- Any additional context that may help the maintainer understand the situation.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible. Information will only be shared when necessary to investigate or address the incident.

Please do not use public issues or pull requests to report sensitive incidents.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will determine appropriate responses to reported violations based on the circumstances, the severity of the behavior, and any relevant history.

Possible responses include:

1. **Correction:** A private or public request to stop the inappropriate behavior.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning that the behavior violates this Code of Conduct.
3. **Temporary restriction:** A temporary limitation on participation in project spaces.
4. **Permanent restriction:** Removal of access to project spaces and exclusion from future participation.

Maintainers reserve the right to take any action they consider necessary to protect the community and maintain a respectful environment.
